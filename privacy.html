Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

rewyndr clipper is a private tool used only by the owner of the @rewyndr.clips social media accounts to publish short video clips to those accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

What it accesses

  • Only the owner's own accounts, after the owner logs in and grants permission.
  • It uses that permission only to upload videos and their titles, descriptions and hashtags to the owner's own channels and profiles.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person.
  • It does not read private messages, contacts or viewer information.
  • It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the owner can log in to it.

Data storage

  • Login tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared.
  • Video files are stored on the owner's computer and deleted automatically a few days after they are posted.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the security settings of each Google, TikTok or Meta account. Revoking access stops all uploads.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the owner through the @rewyndr.clips profile.
